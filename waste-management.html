Waste Management in India

Effective waste management helps reduce pollution, protect natural resources and create cleaner communities. Responsible waste disposal and recycling are important steps towards a sustainable environment in India.

What is Waste Management?

Waste management includes the collection, segregation, recycling and safe disposal of waste generated by households and businesses. It helps minimise environmental damage and encourages responsible resource utilisation.

Types of Waste

1. Biodegradable Waste

Organic waste such as vegetable peels, fruit scraps and garden leaves that can decompose naturally. Suitable biodegradable waste can also be used for composting.

2. Non-Biodegradable Waste

Materials such as plastic, glass and certain metals that take longer to break down. Many of these materials can be reused or recycled.

3. Hazardous Waste

Waste such as certain batteries, chemicals and electronic components that may harm human health and the environment if disposed of improperly.

Importance of Waste Segregation

Separating wet, dry and hazardous waste makes recycling and responsible disposal easier. Waste segregation also reduces the amount of waste sent to landfills.

Simple Waste Management Practices

  • Use separate bins for wet and dry waste.
  • Reuse materials whenever possible.
  • Send recyclable materials for recycling.
  • Compost suitable kitchen waste.
  • Avoid unnecessary single-use plastics.
  • Dispose of hazardous waste through appropriate collection facilities.

Benefits of Proper Waste Management

Proper waste management helps reduce pollution, conserve natural resources, support recycling and maintain cleaner surroundings. It also encourages environmental awareness among individuals and communities.
